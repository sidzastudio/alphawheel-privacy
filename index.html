Word Ripple Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Word Ripple (formerly AlphaWheel, "the app") is a word game published by Sidza Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what information is handled for advertising, usage analytics, and crash reporting.

What we collect

We do not run a server for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your saved game (including your current puzzle, coins, settings, and daily streak) is stored on your device. Your age group and optional reporting choice are also stored locally, separately from your saved game. We do not ask for or store your date of birth. Eligible adults can opt into usage events and diagnostic reports through Google Firebase as described below. The Data retention and your choices section below explains what Reset and uninstalling remove.

Ads

For players who choose “18 or older”, the app can show ads provided by Google AdMob after Google’s privacy checks allow requests: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for a free hint or a bonus, and banner or native ads on selected gameplay and reward screens. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes.

The app asks for non-personalised ads only and limits ads to a general-audience content rating. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to determine whether ads may be requested and to present an ad consent form when required by that service. When required by Google, you can reopen its form from Settings > Ads & data. Settings > Age & privacy is always available in Android release builds. Ads stay off for players who choose Under 18 or Prefer not to say, and while their age choice is unresolved. The game remains playable without ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics and crash reports

In Android release versions, optional reporting is off by default. Players who choose “18 or older” may turn on Share usage and crash reports in Age & privacy. Reporting additionally waits for applicable Google consent; unavailable or denied consent keeps it off. Under-18 and undisclosed-age players do not enable reporting. When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how the game is used, and Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose failures. Analytics records app lifecycle and usage events, including level wins, chapter completion, hints, Buddy clues, rewards, streak milestones, and jar completion. The SDK can collect app-instance identifiers, device/app information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses. We disable advertising ID collection for Analytics and do not enable Analytics advertising storage or personalised-ad use through our reporting controls. We do not attach a player account ID to these events.

Crash reports can include error messages, stack traces, app and device state, installation identifiers, and activity breadcrumbs. We use these reports to identify and fix problems. Google processes this information through Firebase; we can access the resulting reports. Data is encrypted in transit. You can turn optional reporting off or change your age group in Settings > Age & privacy without resetting the game. Reporting eligibility is checked again at each launch. Crash reports may be stored locally; automatic crash uploads stay off, and reports from an eligible previous session may be sent only after the current reporting and consent checks pass. Reports from ineligible sessions are discarded before a later opt-in. Changing an advertising choice also pauses reporting until consent is checked again. Turning reporting off does not delete reports already sent. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Our age choice offers Under 18, 18 or older, and Prefer not to say with no preselected age. Under-18 or undisclosed ages keep ads and optional reporting off. If you believe a child has provided information through an earlier version, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your choices

Reset in Settings clears saved game progress while retaining selected preferences; uninstalling removes local app data, subject to your device backup settings. Neither action deletes reports already sent to Google. Analytics and crash information is retained according to the applicable Firebase and Analytics service settings and policies. Contact us about privacy requests; do not send passwords or sensitive information. Local Reset retains your age and privacy choices; it does not grant consent or delete reports already sent. For reporting and advertising choices, use the controls above and your Google account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, the information in the app's store listing.
