AlphaWheel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

AlphaWheel ("the app") is a word game published by Sidza Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what information is handled when it shows ads.

What we collect

We do not run a server for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress (level, stars, coins, settings and daily streak) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or using Reset in Settings deletes it.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for a free hint or a bonus, and a small banner on some win screens. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes.

The app asks for non-personalised ads only and limits ads to a general-audience content rating. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app shows Google's consent form first, and you can reopen it from Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your choices

Because game data stays on your device, you control it: Reset in Settings or uninstalling removes it. For data held by Google for ads, use your Google account's ad settings and the options above.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, the information in the app's store listing.
