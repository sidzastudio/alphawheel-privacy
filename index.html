Word Ripple Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Word Ripple (formerly AlphaWheel, "the app") is a word game published by Sidza Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what information is handled for advertising, usage analytics, and crash reporting.

What we collect

We do not run a server for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your saved game (including your current puzzle, coins, settings, and daily streak) is stored on your device. Separately, the app sends usage events and diagnostic reports through Google Firebase as described below. The Data retention and your choices section below explains what Reset and uninstalling remove.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for a free hint or a bonus, and banner or native ads on selected gameplay and reward screens. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crashes.

The app asks for non-personalised ads only and limits ads to a general-audience content rating. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to determine whether ads may be requested and to present an ad consent form when required by that service. When available, you can reopen the form from Settings > Privacy options. These ad controls do not themselves switch off Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics and crash reports

Native release versions use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used, and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose failures. Analytics records app lifecycle and usage events, including level wins, chapter completion, hints, Buddy clues, rewards, streak milestones, and jar completion. The SDK can collect app-instance and advertising identifiers, device/app information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses. We do not attach a player account ID to these events.

Crash reports can include error messages, stack traces, app and device state, installation identifiers, and activity breadcrumbs. We use these reports to identify and fix problems. Google processes this information through Firebase; we can access the resulting reports. Data is encrypted in transit. The app currently has no separate in-app switch for Analytics or Crashlytics. Ad privacy choices do not automatically disable this reporting. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and your choices

Reset in Settings clears saved game progress while retaining selected preferences; uninstalling removes local app data, subject to your device backup settings. Neither action deletes reports already sent to Google. Analytics and crash information is retained according to the applicable Firebase and Analytics service settings and policies. Contact us about privacy requests; do not send passwords or sensitive information. For advertising choices, use the ad privacy options above and your Google account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, the information in the app's store listing.
